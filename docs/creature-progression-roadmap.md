# Creature progression roadmap

Recall Forge treats the creature as a maths companion, never a dependent pet. Progress should celebrate useful learning behaviours without punishment, guilt, paid currency, random purchases, or pressure to maintain a streak.

## Foundation in V0.11

- Levels continue beyond the five visual evolution stages.
- Champion ranks are earned every five levels after Level 11.
- Rank changes have a visible aura and a clear milestone message.
- The next rank and the XP still needed are shown on the home and profile screens.
- Energy still describes readiness for training and never drops below the safe resting level.

## Recommended long-term layers

### 1. Branching Champion forms

At Levels 16, 26, and 41, let the child choose one of two visual paths for their species. Paths should reflect learning preferences, such as Explorer, Builder, or Scholar, and never make one path stronger than another. Store the choice so it remains part of the creature's identity.

### 2. Habitats that grow with learning

Unlock small habitat scenes through broad milestones: securing a full table, completing a memory test cycle, practising division, or revisiting facts after four weeks. Decorations should be chosen from a collection rather than awarded through chance.

### 3. Maths mastery badges

Add permanent badges for durable achievements such as "7x table remembered after 4 weeks" or "division facts secured". Prefer retention and improvement milestones over raw speed, perfect scores, or daily attendance.

### 4. Story chapters

Release short, optional chapters every few Champion ranks. A chapter can introduce a new habitat, cosmetic set, or training quest theme. It should take a few weeks of normal use to complete and should never expire.

### 5. Age progression

Keep the early presentation bright and direct. Later ranks can offer a more capable, less childish profile layout, richer creature forms, and harder mixed-operation quests without deleting earlier rewards. An admin setting can select a presentation band while preserving all progress.

### 6. Seasonal collections without resets

Optional seasonal cosmetics can return every year and remain permanently available once earned. Do not reset levels, remove creatures, create scarcity pressure, or require consecutive attendance.

## Suggested delivery sequence

1. Add three Champion aura and accessory sets using the existing species assets.
2. Add one branching form choice at Level 16 and persist it per profile.
3. Add habitat selection and five learning-based habitat unlocks.
4. Add retention badges tied to baseline, four-week, and eight-week memory checks.
5. Pilot one three-part story chapter before building more content.

This sequence provides several years of visible progress while keeping the learning engine, data model, and child-facing promises understandable.
